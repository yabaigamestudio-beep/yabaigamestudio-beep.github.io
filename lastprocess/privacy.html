《最後進程》（以下稱「本遊戲」）重視你的隱私。

  • 遊戲本身不收集個人資料：本遊戲不會要求或收集你的姓名、電子郵件、位置或聯絡人。
  • 廣告：本遊戲使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能會收集並使用裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID）、IP 位址、大略位置與廣告互動資料，用於顯示廣告、衡量成效與防止詐騙。詳見 Google 的隱私權政策，以及 Google 如何使用合作夥伴應用程式的資料。
  • 同意與選擇：在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，首次啟動時會詢問你是否同意個人化廣告，之後可在「設定 > 關於 > 廣告隱私選項」隨時更改。你也可以在 Android 的「設定 > Google > 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。
  • 遊戲進度只存在你的裝置上：設定與進度儲存在本遊戲的私有資料夾中，不會上傳，解除安裝時會一併刪除。
  • 權限：網路（載入廣告）、廣告 ID（由 AdMob 使用）、震動（受到攻擊時的回饋，可在設定中關閉）。
  • 兒童：本遊戲不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。

English

Last Process ("the game") respects your privacy.

  • The game itself collects no personal data: it never asks for or collects your name, email, location or contacts.
  • Ads: the game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Consent and choices: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads on first launch; you can change it any time in Settings > About > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Progress stays on your device: settings and progress are saved in the game's private folder, never uploaded, and removed when you uninstall.
  • Permissions: internet (to load ads), advertising ID (used by AdMob), vibration (haptic feedback when you take damage; can be turned off in Settings).
  • Children: the game is not directed at children under 13.
