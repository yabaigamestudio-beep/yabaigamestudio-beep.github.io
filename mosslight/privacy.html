苔光方塊隱私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日
開發者：Aprilwave Studio
適用遊戲：苔光方塊 / Mosslight Blocks（com.mosslight.blocks）
聯絡：yabaigamestudio@gmail.com

目前遊戲為測試版，使用測試廣告版位，真實購買停用。廣告 SDK 仍可能處理下述資料；正式啟用廣告與購買前，我們會更新適用設定、商店揭露及本政策。

遊戲進度與本機資料

遊玩不需建立遊戲帳號，也不要求提供姓名或生日。棋盤、分數、收藏、設定、語言、每日完成紀錄及獎勵額度存在裝置中，沒有自建雲端同步。移除應用或清除應用資料可能刪除進度；作業系統備份取決於裝置設定，不保證恢復。

廣告與第三方服務

遊戲整合 Google AdMob 與 User Messaging Platform。依 SDK、地區及同意選擇，Google 可能收集與分享 IP 位址（可用於推估概略位置）、裝置或廣告識別資料、廣告互動與診斷資訊，用於廣告、分析及防詐欺。資料會由 Google 的服務處理，並非全都只存在你的裝置。

在適用情況下，玩家可從遊戲設定開啟廣告隱私選項。獎勵廣告由玩家自選；不觀看不會扣除原有成果或阻止核心遊玩。Google 的資料保留與權利處理由其政策及相關設定管理：Google 隱私政策、Google Mobile Ads 資料揭露。

永久去廣告與交易

正式版預計提供一次性永久去廣告，由 Google Play 處理交易，遊戲依商店回傳狀態提供及恢復權益，並在本機快取權益。開發者不取得完整付款卡號。交易紀錄、退款及商店保留規則由 Google Play 處理。恢復購買只處理去廣告權益，不保證恢復棋盤與收藏。目前測試版沒有真實付款。

自願的本機測試紀錄

測試紀錄預設關閉，只有玩家在設定中開啟才會記錄對局事件、數值、語言及效能。只存本機，最多約兩份各 256 KiB，舊檔輪替，不會自動上傳。不在紀錄中寫入完整棋盤存檔、付款收據、信箱、廣告 ID 或裝置唯一識別碼。可關閉後續記錄，並在同一頁刪除紀錄。

玩家可自行複製包含版本、系統、機型、螢幕及效能的診斷摘要。客服郵件由玩家檢視後自行寄出，不會自動附加完整存檔或紀錄。

客服、保留與刪除

若你主動聯絡客服，我們會收到你提供的信箱、問題與附件，只用於回覆、除錯及處理你的請求。我們僅在處理問題所需期間保存，結案後刪除不再需要的資料；依法須保留者限於必要範圍。請不要寄送密碼、付款卡號或驗證碼。

可透過上述信箱詢問、請求更正或刪除你主動提供的客服資料，請只提供辨識該次來信所需資訊。本機資料由你控制；刪除測試紀錄不會刪除遊戲進度，清除全部應用資料則可能永久遺失進度。我們無法替你刪除由 Google 獨立處理的資料，可使用 Google 的隱私控制與請求管道。

兒童與家庭

遊戲以全年齡使用為規劃方向。正式面向包含兒童的受眾發布前，仍須完成相應的廣告、SDK 與隱私流程設定；本政策不表示測試版已通過家庭政策審核。家長若認為孩子透過客服提供了不必要的個人資料，可聯絡我們要求刪除。

本網站與政策更新

本網站使用 GitHub Pages，沒有加入分析工具、廣告或自行設置的追蹤 Cookie。GitHub 會為安全目的記錄網站訪客 IP，詳見 GitHub 隱私聲明。網站語言選單只在頁面切換文字，不保存偏好。我們會在本頁公布政策變更並更新日期；如資料處理有重大改變，亦會依適用要求提供說明或取得同意。

Mosslight Blocks Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: 29 October 2026
Developer: Aprilwave Studio
Game: Mosslight Blocks / 苔光方塊 (com.mosslight.blocks)
Contact: yabaigamestudio@gmail.com

The current game is a test build using test ad units, with real purchases disabled. Ad SDKs may still process the data described below. Before enabling production ads and purchases, we will update applicable settings, store disclosures and this policy.

Progress and local data

No game account, name or date of birth is required to play. Boards, scores, collection, settings, language, daily completion records and reward allowances are saved on your device. We do not operate cloud save synchronization. Uninstalling or clearing app data may erase progress. Operating-system backup depends on your device and is not guaranteed to restore progress.

Ads and third-party services

The game integrates Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform. Depending on SDK settings, region and consent, Google may collect and share IP addresses (which may infer approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This data is processed by Google's services and does not all stay on your device.

Where applicable, ad privacy options are available in game Settings. Rewarded ads are optional: declining does not remove earned progress or block core gameplay. Google's retention practices and privacy controls are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Ad removal and transactions

The production version is planned to offer a one-time permanent ad-removal purchase processed by Google Play. The game uses store-returned purchase status to grant and restore access, with a local entitlement cache. We do not receive full payment card numbers. Google Play handles transaction records, refunds and store retention rules. Restoring purchases restores ad-free access, not a guaranteed recovery of boards or collection. Real payments are unavailable in the current test build.

Optional local playtest records

Recording is off by default and starts only if you enable it in Settings. It records play events, run statistics, language and performance locally, in at most about two rotating files of 256 KiB each. It never uploads itself. It excludes full board saves, payment receipts, email addresses, advertising IDs and unique device identifiers. You can stop recording and delete these records in the same settings page.

You may choose to copy a diagnostic summary containing game version, operating system, device model, screen dimensions and performance. You review and send support email yourself; full saves and logs are not automatically attached.

Support, retention and deletion

If you contact support, we receive the email address, description and attachments you choose to send, for responding, troubleshooting and handling your request. We keep these only while needed to resolve the matter and delete information no longer necessary after resolution, except limited retention required by law. Never send passwords, payment card numbers or verification codes.

Contact the address above to ask about, correct or request deletion of support data you provided, using only the information needed to identify your correspondence. You control local app data. Deleting playtest records leaves game progress intact; clearing all app data may permanently remove progress. We cannot delete data Google processes independently; use Google's privacy controls and request channels.

Children and families

The game is being planned for all-age use. Appropriate ad, SDK and privacy settings must still be completed before production distribution to an audience including children. This policy does not mean the test build has passed Families review. Parents may contact us to request deletion of unnecessary personal data a child sent through support.

This website and updates

This site uses GitHub Pages, without added analytics, advertising or our own tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The introduction language selector changes page text without saving a preference. We publish policy updates here with a revised date and provide notice or request consent for material changes where required.
